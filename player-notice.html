You are reading this because the Roblox experience you are playing uses {{service}}, a moderation tool provided by {{company}} and run by the experience's creators ("the operators"). It helps them keep the game safe and fair.

What happens while you play

  • The game sends your Roblox user ID, username and display name to the moderation service when you join or leave.
  • It records how long you play and any points you earn.
  • Messages you send in the game's main public chat are checked by moderators. They are held temporarily, and a message is stored only if it contains a word the operators flagged or if a player reports the chat. Private and team chat are not monitored.
  • If a moderator warns, mutes, kicks or bans you, a record is kept with the reason and date.
  • You can send a report to moderators by typing !modcall followed by your message.

How long we keep it

Reports and the chat attached to them are deleted after {{callRetentionDays}} days. Other records are kept until deleted on request or by the operators. See the Privacy Policy for details.
