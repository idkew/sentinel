This policy applies to everyone who uses {{service}}, including staff and game operators. It exists to keep the Service safe and fair for the communities that depend on it.

You must not

  • Use moderation tools to harass, bully or retaliate against someone, or to punish people because of their race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, nationality or other protected characteristic.

  • Access, copy or share player data without authority, or use it for anything other than moderating and running your experience (for example advertising, profiling, selling or doxxing).

  • Use the Service to break Roblox's rules or any law, or to put children at risk.
  • Upload malicious code or tamper with another operator's experience.

You should

  • Give clear reasons for moderation actions and keep fair records.
  • Give players a way to appeal and respond to their requests.
  • Give staff only the role they need.
