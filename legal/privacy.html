{{company}} ("we", "us") provides {{service}}, a moderation and community tool for Roblox experiences. This policy explains what personal information is handled when staff use the dashboard and when players play an experience whose operator has installed {{service}}. Players, see also our short Player Notice.

1. Our role

For staff accounts and use of the dashboard, we decide how data is used (we are the "controller"). For player data, the experience operator (the person or team running the game) decides that moderation happens and we process the data on their behalf (we are their "processor"/"service provider"). Operators are responsible for their own legal obligations to their players.

We do not collect passwords for Roblox, payment details of players, precise location, or contacts. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

3. How we use it

To run the service, enforce moderation decisions made by operators, keep the service secure, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations. We do not use player data to profile or market to players.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Staff data: performing our contract with you and our legitimate interest in security. Player data: the operator's and our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe, and compliance with legal obligations. Where an operator relies on consent, they are responsible for obtaining it.

5. Who we share it with

  • Infrastructure providers that host the service and store its data for us (for example our web host and database provider), under contract.
  • Roblox: to look up users and apply bans, the service contacts Roblox's public APIs and ban system. Roblox then handles that information under its own privacy policy. When you view the dashboard, your browser loads avatar images from Roblox's content network.
  • Google and Roblox: when you sign in with either, they learn that you are using {{service}} and receive your IP address, and they tell us your name and ID. They handle that under their own privacy policies.
  • Discord: if a workspace admin connects a Discord channel, short summaries of events they choose (for example a ban with the player's name, the reason and the staff member) are posted to that channel and handled under Discord's policies.
  • Authorities or advisers where required by law or to protect rights and safety, and a successor if our business is transferred.

6. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than yours. Where required we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Security

We never see your Google or Roblox password. Sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies; access is limited by role and each game's data is kept separate from every other game's; each game has its own secret key; and requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Tell us promptly if you suspect a problem.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your data, to receive a copy, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents have the rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing; we do not sell or share personal information. We can export and erase a player's stored data on request and aim to respond within 30 days. Contact us at {{email}}. Players may also ask the experience's operator.

10. Children

Many Roblox players are children. Staff accounts are for people aged 13 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for any purpose other than the moderation and safety functions described here, which operators enable in their own experiences. Operators must provide any notices and obtain any parental consent that the law requires for their players. A parent or guardian who has a concern can contact us at the address below and we will help locate and delete the data.
