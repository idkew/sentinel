The short version: this game's team uses a moderation tool to keep players safe. It knows your Roblox name and how long you play, and it looks at public chat for rule-breaking. You can ask to see or delete your information. We do not sell it or use it for ads.

You are reading this because the Roblox experience you are playing uses {{service}}, a moderation tool provided by {{company}} and run by the experience's creators ("the operators"). It helps them keep the game safe and fair. This notice is written to be easy to read. Parents and guardians are welcome to read it too.

What happens while you play

  • The game sends your Roblox user ID, username and display name to the moderation service when you join or leave, and which server you are in.
  • It records how long you play and any points you earn.
  • Messages you send in the game's main public chat are checked by moderators. They are held temporarily (never more than 24 hours), and a message is stored longer only if it contains a word the operators flagged, if a player reports the chat, or if a moderator saves it with a warning, kick, mute or ban. Private chat and team chat are not monitored.
  • If a moderator warns, mutes, kicks or bans you, a record is kept with the reason, the date and who did it. Moderators can add links to clips or screenshots and private notes to a record.
  • The tool may show moderators a hint that a new player could be someone who was banned before (for example if the name is very similar, or the Roblox account is very new). This is only a hint to look closer. Nothing happens to you automatically because of it.
  • You can send a report to moderators by typing !modcall followed by your message. The report keeps a few of the latest chat messages so moderators can see what happened.

Who can see it

Only the moderators and admins of this game, with the access the operators give them. Different games using {{service}} cannot see each other's information. Our hosting providers store the data for us under contract. We only share information elsewhere if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety. If the operators have connected Discord, short summaries of moderation events (such as a ban, with the name, reason and staff member) are posted to their Discord server. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

If you are banned

  • Your ban stays active until it ends or the operators lift it.
  • If the operators allow appeals, you can use their public appeal page. You give your Roblox username and explain what happened. You get a private link to check the result and read any reply.
  • Moderators decide appeals, not {{company}}. We make the tool, but the operators make the moderation decisions in their game.

How long we keep it

  • Live chat: never more than 24 hours, unless it was flagged, reported or saved with a record.
  • Reports and the chat attached to them: deleted after {{callRetentionDays}} days.
  • Playtime and game joins: about six months.
  • Moderation records and points: kept until deleted on request or by the operators.
  • Backups: deleted after 7 days.

Your choices

  • You (or your parent or guardian) can ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you, or ask for a moderation decision to be reviewed, by contacting the experience's operators or {{company}} at {{email}}.
  • Tell us your Roblox username and the name of the game. We may ask you to prove the account is yours.
  • We aim to answer within 30 days. We may need to keep some records, for example an active ban, where that is needed to keep the community safe or the law requires it. We will tell you if so.
  • We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or build marketing profiles of players. Moderators, not computers, make moderation decisions.

You are not alone

If something in a game worries or upsets you, tell a trusted adult. You can also report it to Roblox using the report button in the game.

Suggested in-game text for operators

"This game uses moderation tools. Your username, playtime, public chat and any moderation actions are processed to keep players safe. Read more at [link to this page]."

Operators: put this text, or something like it, where players will see it, for example in your game description or on a sign in the lobby.
