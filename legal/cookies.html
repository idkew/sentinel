3. Requests to other companies

  • Google and Roblox sign-in. When you choose Continue with Google or Continue with Roblox, you leave our site and they set their own cookies under their own policies. They learn that you are using {{service}}.
  • Roblox content network. Avatar images are loaded straight from Roblox, which receives your IP address and browser details when your browser asks for them.
  • Fonts. The pages may load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it does.

We do not control cookies set by other companies. Please see their policies.

4. Consent

Strictly necessary cookies are needed to give you the service you asked for, and do not need consent in most places, so we do not show a cookie banner. Preference items only remember your own choices on this device. If we ever add optional cookies, for example for analytics, we will ask you first and update this page before we do.

5. Your choices

  • You can delete or block cookies and clear local and session storage in your browser settings. If you block the strictly necessary ones you will not be able to stay signed in.
  • Signing out removes your sign-in cookies. Deleting sv, sa and the local storage items just means those notices and choices come back.
  • We do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, because we do not track you across other sites.
