The short version: use {{service}} to keep your game safe and fair, not to hurt people, spy on them or break things. Be fair, be honest and keep other people's information private.

This policy applies to everyone who uses {{service}} (the "Service"), including game owners, administrators, moderators and anyone who uses a public page such as an appeal or application form. It is part of our Terms of Service. It exists to keep the Service safe and fair for the communities that depend on it, including the many young people who play on Roblox.

1. Using moderation tools fairly

You must not:

  • Use moderation tools to harass, bully, stalk, intimidate or take revenge on someone, or to punish people because of their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexuality, disability, age, nationality or other protected characteristic.
  • Ban, mute, kick, warn or report people in bad faith, or make up reports, evidence, appeals or application answers.
  • Punish someone only because of an automatic hint, such as a possible alt account, without checking it yourself.
  • Ignore a fair appeal because of a personal dispute, or use appeals to humiliate people.

2. Protecting people's information

You must not:

  • Access, copy, share, sell or publish player or staff information (including chat, evidence, form answers and appeals) without authority, or use it for anything other than moderating and running your experience. Examples of misuse include advertising, profiling, selling, "doxxing", public shaming and trading information with other groups.
  • Try to work out where someone lives, who they are in real life, or contact them outside the game about a moderation matter in a way that scares or pressures them.
  • Collect information about people whom you have no moderation reason to track.
  • Keep information you no longer need, or refuse a valid request to see or delete someone's data.

3. Protecting accounts and the Service

You must not:

  • Share your sign-in, game key, Roblox API key or invite links with people who should not have them, or use someone else's account.
  • Give anyone more power than they need, or hand out administrator rights to people you do not know and trust.
  • Try to break, overload, probe, scan or bypass the Service, its rate limits, its two-step verification, its login protections or its permissions, or reverse engineer it to attack it.
  • Access another workspace or operator's data, or tamper with another operator's experience.
  • Upload malicious code, or modify the game script so that it hides actions, forges records or sends data somewhere it should not go.
  • Use bots, scrapers or automated tools on the Service other than the game script we provide.

4. Messages, forms and announcements

You must not use announcements, messages, replies, forms, appeals or any other part of the Service to spam, deceive, phish, scam, impersonate someone, or send adult, hateful, violent, threatening or illegal content. Do not use a form to collect passwords, security codes or payment information from anyone.

5. Roblox, the law and children

You must not use the Service to break Roblox's rules or any law, or to put children at risk. In particular:

  • Follow Roblox's Terms of Use, Community Standards and developer rules.
  • Do not use the Service to groom, exploit or target children, or to ask children for personal information, photos or contact details.
  • If you see content or behaviour that puts a child in danger, report it to Roblox and, where the law requires or the risk is serious, to the authorities. Moderation records in {{service}} do not replace reporting.

6. What we expect of you

  • Give clear reasons for moderation actions and keep fair records.
  • Give players a way to appeal, read appeals honestly and reply.
  • Give staff only the role they need, and remove access when someone leaves or misuses it.
  • Treat what you see in the dashboard as confidential.
  • Tell us at {{email}} about security problems instead of using them, and about misuse you see.

7. Reporting misuse

If you think someone is breaking this policy, for example a moderator abusing their power, email {{email}} with what happened, the name of the experience and any evidence you have. Please do not include other people's private details that we do not need. For a decision about your own moderation, contact the experience's operators first. Operators decide moderation in their own game, and we cannot reverse their decisions, but we can act if the Service is being misused.

8. Enforcement

If we think this policy has been broken we may, depending on how serious it is and whether it is a first time: ask you to stop, remove content, limit features, suspend or end access to the Service or a workspace, and, where required, report to Roblox, the authorities or the people affected. We will tell you what we did and why where we reasonably can, and you can reply to ask us to look again. We may act straight away without warning where there is a risk to a child or other person, to the Service or to the law. See the Terms of Service.
